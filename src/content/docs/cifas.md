[Cifas](https://www.cifas.org.uk/) is the UK's fraud-prevention organisation, and its members
(e.g. Banks, Loan providers, Insurers, Mortgage providers) use information from their 
[National Fraud Database](https://www.cifas.org.uk/fraud-prevention-community/combined-threat-protect/national-fraud-database)
to help assess fraud risk when applications for financial products are made.

A Cifas record is **not the same thing as a [credit rating](https://ukpersonal.finance/credit-ratings/)
or a County Court Judgment (CCJ)**. Depending on the type of record, it may either be a warning
intended to protect you from fraud, or a mark indicating that an organisation believes you have been
involved in fraudulent activity.

## The three types you are most likely to encounter

### Protective Registration

You can voluntarily register for Cifas **Protective Registration** if you are concerned that your
personal details are at risk of identity fraud. There are countless examples in the history of 
[r/UKPersonalFinance](https://www.reddit.com/r/UKPersonalFinance) of family members fraudulently taking out
credit cards without the knowledge of the person named and running up hidden debts; Protective Registration
can help reduce this risk. [For a small fee](https://www.cifas.org.uk/pr), it places a warning against 
your details in the database for **2 years**. When a Cifas member sees your details being used to
apply for a product or service, they are prompted to carry out additional checks to establish that
the application is legitimate.

### Victim of Impersonation

A **Victim of Impersonation** marker is used when an organisation believes that someone has attempted 
to use your identity fraudulently. It tells other Cifas members that you are the genuine, innocent
party and that additional validation is required. Victim of Impersonation warnings 
[remain in place for **13 months**](https://www.cifas.org.uk/services/identity-protection/victim-of-impersonation).

:::note Protective Registration and Victim of Impersonation markers are fraud-prevention measures,
not an accusation of fraud. :::

### Fraud marker

A **Fraud Marker** is recorded when a Cifas member believes that an individual has been involved in
fraudulent conduct. Unlike Protective Registration and Victim of Impersonation, this is an adverse 
record and can make obtaining financial products and other services considerably more difficult;
for example being unable to access bank accounts with overdraft facilities, credit cards or loans.

Fraud markers may last **up to 6 years**.

## Finding out whether you have a marker

You can request your information **for free** from Cifas by raising a 
[Data Subject Access Request](https://www.cifas.org.uk/dsar). If you believe a record is incorrect,
you can challenge it through the [dispute process](https://www.cifas.org.uk/contact-us/i-want-to-make-a-complaint).
