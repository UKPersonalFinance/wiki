The flowchart and wiki were written by members of the UKPF community on
Reddit and Discord. If you have any questions about what you've read
here, want to help others improve their finances, or just want to chat
about personal finances topics, you are very much invited to join in!

- [Discord invite link](https://discord.gg/kaetMg8)
- [Reddit: r/UKPersonalFinance](https://www.reddit.com/r/UKPersonalFinance/)

If you haven't used these platforms before, Discord works like a group
chat that you can join to talk to people who are online in real time. On
Reddit you can write posts and respond to other people's.

Both communities are very active and welcoming.
